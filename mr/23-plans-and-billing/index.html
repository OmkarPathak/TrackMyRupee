    Plans and billing¶

    What each plan includes¶

    Free Plus Pro
    Expenses per month 90 Unlimited Unlimited
    Active accounts 2 10 Unlimited
    Budget categories 5 15 Unlimited
    Active subscriptions (recurring) 2 5 Unlimited
    Savings goals 1 5 Unlimited
    Loans Not included 1 active loan Unlimited
    Net worth history 2 months 6 months Full
    Alert emails No Yes Yes
    Year in Review No Yes Yes

    Exporting your data is free on every plan. Prices are on the pricing page. The Free plan never expires.

    Two ways to pay¶

    One-time Auto-renewing subscription
    What you pick Buy 1 Month or Buy 1 Year (auto-renew switched off) Subscribe (auto-renew switched on)
    Renews itself No Yes, each month or year until you cancel
    Reminder before it ends Yes, two days before No, because it renews
    • Paying while you still have time left adds the new period after the end of the time you already paid for, so nothing is lost.
    • Paying during the 7-day free Pro trial gives you the whole period you paid for. The trial days do not shorten it.
    • Upgrading from Plus to Pro switches your plan straight away. The time you already paid for is kept, and the new period is added to it.
    • You can have one auto-renewing subscription at a time, so you are never billed twice. To change plan, cancel the current subscription first (from Settings → Payment history; access continues to the end of the period you paid for) or buy a one-time period.

    You get a thank-you email after your first payment on a plan. See Emails.

    Free trial¶

    A Free account that has never had a trial can start a 7-day Pro trial from the pricing page, with no card needed. It is offered once.

    Cancelling¶

    Open Settings → Payment history and choose Cancel Subscription. Access continues until the end of the period you have already paid for, and nothing more is charged. A one-time payment has nothing to cancel: it simply ends on its end date.

    If a renewal payment keeps failing, the subscription stops and the account returns to Free.

    When a plan ends¶

    Your data is never deleted. The account behaves as Free from the moment the plan ends:

    • If you are over a limit, your oldest accounts, categories, subscriptions and goals stay usable and the newer ones are locked until you upgrade or delete some.
    • Loans and Year in Review need Plus or Pro again. Exporting your data stays available on every plan.

    Your payment history is under Settings → Payment history.
