{% trans "Data at Rest" %}

{% trans "Our database is a managed PostgreSQL service hosted on Supabase in Mumbai, India. Supabase encrypts stored data at rest (storage-level encryption). TrackMyRupee does not add a second layer of encryption inside the app, so the app itself, and authorised administrators of the database, can read the data. Please avoid entering highly sensitive information, such as bank account or card numbers, in descriptions." %}

{% trans "Data in Transit" %}

{% trans "We serve the site over HTTPS, and the app is configured to connect to its database over TLS. This protects your data from being read or altered while it travels between your browser, our servers and the database." %}

{# The owner confirmed on 2026-10-13 that no Supabase backups are configured, so no backup claims are made. If backups are enabled, describe their frequency and retention here and update privacy_policy.html section 7. #}

{% trans "Backups" %}

{% trans "We do not currently configure database backups. There is no separate copy of your data to restore from if the database is lost or corrupted, so we encourage you to use the CSV export from time to time to keep your own copy. Because there are no backups, data you delete is removed from our live database immediately and is not kept elsewhere." %}

{% trans "Family Sharing" %}

{% trans "Sharing is off until you turn it on, account by account. Other family members receive only a fixed set of fields for the accounts you choose, never your transactions, notes, categories, receipts, the holdings inside an account or any account numbers. Invitation links work once, expire after 7 days and are stored only as a hash. An emergency sheet says what exists and where to look, never account numbers or passwords, By default it opens only on request, after a delay you can veto; if you choose, the people you name can open it at any time. Leaving the family ends all sharing straight away. We test this with automated checks that plant private data and confirm no family page, reply, email or notification shows it." %}

{% trans "Privacy First" %}

{% trans "We are an indie-developed tool built to provide value, not to farm data. We explicitly pledge never to sell your personal or financial data to third-party advertisers or brokers. Insights and forecasts are generated by our own application logic; your transaction data is not sent to third-party AI services. You maintain ownership of your data at all times." %}
