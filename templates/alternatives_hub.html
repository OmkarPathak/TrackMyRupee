{% trans "The three kinds of finance trackers in India" %}

  • {% trans "1. SMS auto-trackers" %}

    {% trans "They read bank SMS alerts and log transactions for you. Least effort, but the app needs permission to read your message inbox." %}

  • {% trans "2. Email and account-linking apps" %}

    {% trans "They pull investments and balances by scanning your email or linking accounts. Good for a single portfolio view, but they need access to your inbox or financial credentials." %}

  • {% trans "3. Manual, privacy-first trackers" %}

    {% trans "You log spends yourself, usually in seconds. A little more effort, but no one reads your messages or email. TrackMyRupee is in this group." %}

{% trans "What to check before choosing" %}

  • {% trans "Permissions: does it need SMS, email or bank-login access?" %}
  • {% trans "Net worth: can it track mutual funds, FDs, PPF, stocks and loans, not just daily spending?" %}
  • {% trans "Salary-cycle budgeting: does your month start on payday, not on the 1st?" %}
  • {% trans "Data control: can you export everything as CSV whenever you want?" %}
  • {% trans "Business model: ads and cross-selling, or paid subscriptions?" %}
  • {% trans "Language: English, Hindi and Marathi support." %}
