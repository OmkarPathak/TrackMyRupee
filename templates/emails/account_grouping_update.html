We are thrilled to announce a major upgrade to how you track and visualize your wealth on TrackMyRupee. We are moving away from our flat legacy account types to a smart, Grouped Account System with per-type valuation engines.

Why are we switching?

Previously, all accounts were treated flatly. This means your volatile mutual funds, revolving credit cards, vehicle depreciation, and daily liquid cash were all processed using the exact same logic. This caused distortions in your monthly savings rate and created clutter on your dashboard.

What's New in the Grouped System:
  • Clarity with Accordions: Accounts are now neatly categorized into logical tabs, letting you review your short-term cash flow separately from long-term assets.
  • Smart Valuation Calculations: Market-linked assets use unit-NAV valuation, meaning market swings update your Net Worth automatically without corrupting your monthly budget or savings rates.
  • Flawless Loan Tracking: Repayments now hook into the double-entry ledger, automatically splitting into principal reduction (transfer) and interest burn (expense).

How to Migrate Your Legacy Accounts (Step-by-Step)

To take full advantage of the new valuation engine and keep your financial reports clean, follow these quick steps:

  1. Go to your Account List: Navigate to the "Accounts" page in the menu bar.
  2. Locate Legacy Accounts: Look under the Legacy accordion section. These are marked with labels like "Cash (legacy)" or "Bank Account (legacy)".
  3. Click Edit: Click the three-dot actions menu on the right side of the legacy account and select Edit.
  4. Select a Precise Type: In the searchable "Account Type" dropdown, search for and select the corresponding modern type. For example, change "Bank Account (legacy)" to "Savings Account" or "Salary Account".
  5. Save Changes: Click Save Account. The account will immediately move into its new accordion group and start using its optimized valuation rules.
  6. Note: Once you change the account type, there will be no effect on any historical transactions. This is just a cosmetic change.

The 6 Account Groups Explained

1. Cash & Bank
Liquid funds (Savings, Salary, Cash) that contribute directly to your day-to-day spending power.
2. Fixed-Income
Low-risk assets (FD, RD, PPF, EPF) showing interest accruals and employer contributions.
3. Investments
Market-linked assets (Mutual Funds, Demat, ETF, Gold) tracked dynamically via NAV.
4. Short-Term Credit
Revolving debts (Credit Cards, BNPL) that act as immediate liabilities reducing Net Worth.
5. Long-Term Loans
Linked loan contracts (Home/Personal Loans) tracking your outstanding principal.
6. Physical Assets
Real Estate or Vehicles that track depreciation/appreciation over time.

How this improves your metrics:

  • Net Worth: Net Worth = Asset Balances - (Short-Term Credit + Loan Outstanding Principals).
  • Savings Rate: Properly calculated as percentage of net income saved, isolated from asset-class transfer reallocations (like SIPs).
  • Daily Burn Pace: More realistic tracking of how much you are spending per day relative to your monthly budget limits.
