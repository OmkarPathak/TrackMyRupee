TrackMyRupee is one place for your whole financial life: everyday spending, every account and card, loans, goals and your net worth. It is built for Indian money, but works in any currency.

YOUR FIRST 5 MINUTES
1. Add your accounts (bank, cash, cards, wallets, deposits, investments) with their current balances.
2. Log your first expense. Just type something like "swiggy 320" in the quick-add box and press Enter.
3. Add your salary or other income, so your savings rate can be worked out.
4. Set a budget for the one category you want to keep an eye on.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

Track every rupee
- Expenses and income, with categories, notes and payment methods.
- Quick-add: type a line, or tap the mic, and the details are filled in for you.
- Upload past expenses from Excel and get months of history in seconds.
- Recurring transactions for rent, salary, subscriptions and SIPs, logged for you on the due date.

See the whole picture
- Accounts: bank, cash, credit cards, wallets, fixed and recurring deposits, mutual funds, stocks and more, each with its own ledger.
- Transfers between accounts, which never count as spending.
- Net worth: everything you own minus everything you owe, as it changes over time.
- Loans: EMIs split into principal and interest, with the interest counted as the cost of borrowing.
- Capital events: one-off big items (a laptop, a wedding, a down payment) tracked separately, so one large purchase does not distort your monthly picture.

Stay in control
- Budgets by category, with a progress bar and an alert when you get close.
- Savings goals with progress and milestone alerts at 50%, 90% and 100%.
- Calendar view: spending day by day, to spot the expensive days.
- Reminders: bills and credit card dates 3 days ahead, and a daily nudge to log your expenses (you can switch it off in Settings).

Understand your money
- Dashboard and Analytics: savings rate, where your money goes, trends month on month and a financial health view.
- Search and filters on every list, the same way everywhere.
- Year in Review (Plus and Pro): your year in one page.

Take it with you
- Works in the browser on any phone, and installs to your home screen like an app. Push notifications are available once you allow them.
- Export your data to CSV or Excel (Plus and Pro). It is your data.
