TrackMyRupee is one place for your whole financial life: everyday spending, every account and card, loans, goals and your net worth. It is built for Indian money, but works in any currency.

Your first 5 minutes

  1. Add your accounts (bank, cash, cards, wallets) with their current balances.
  2. Log your first expense: type something like swiggy 320 in the quick-add box and press Enter.
  3. Add your salary or other income, so your savings rate can be worked out.
  4. Set a budget for the one category you want to watch.
Go to Dashboard

What you can do

⚡ Track every rupee: expenses and income with categories, quick-add by typing or voice, and upload past expenses from Excel.

🔄 Recurring transactions: rent, salary, subscriptions and SIPs logged for you on the due date.

🏦 Accounts and transfers: bank, cash, cards, wallets, deposits and investments, each with its own ledger. Transfers never count as spending.

📈 Net worth: everything you own minus everything you owe, over time.

🏠 Loans and capital events: EMIs split into principal and interest, and one-off big purchases kept apart from your monthly picture.

🎯 Budgets and goals: category budgets with alerts, and savings goals with milestones at 50%, 90% and 100%.

📅 Calendar and analytics: spending day by day, your savings rate, month-on-month trends and a financial health view.

🔔 Reminders: bills and card dates 3 days ahead, plus a daily nudge to log expenses (switch it off in Settings).

📱 On your phone: install it to your home screen like an app, and export your data any time, on every plan.
