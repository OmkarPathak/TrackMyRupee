{% trans "3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies" %}

{% blocktrans %} We always use a few strictly necessary cookies: your login session, CSRF protection, and a record of your cookie choice. Analytics cookies and scripts are used only if you click Accept on the cookie banner. If you decline, or have not chosen yet, the Google Analytics and PostHog scripts are not loaded, no analytics cookies are set, and no analytics events are sent from your browser or from our servers. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Google Analytics (only if you accept)" %}

{% blocktrans %} Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information such as your IP address, browser type, device and the pages you visit, so we can understand how the site is used. We switch off Google's advertising features. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %} You can also opt out of Google Analytics across all websites by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. {% endblocktrans %} Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

{% trans "PostHog (only if you accept)" %}

{% blocktrans %} PostHog helps us understand which features (such as budgets, goals, and exports) are used. It receives page views, feature events (for example, that a budget was created), your plan tier, and a pseudonymous identifier, which is a salted hash of your account ID. We do not send your email, username, transaction descriptions or amounts to PostHog. Automatic click and text capture and session recording are turned off. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Changing your choice" %}

{% blocktrans %} You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie preferences link in the footer, or the button below. Declining removes the analytics cookies we set. Analytics data already collected is not deleted when you change your choice. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "5. Data Security" %}

{% blocktrans %} We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information. {% endblocktrans %}

  • {% blocktrans %} Passwords are securely hashed using industry-standard algorithms (PBKDF2) and are never stored in plain text. {% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans %} Secure HTTPS (TLS/SSL) for all data transmitted between your browser and our servers. {% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans %} Payment processing is handled entirely by Razorpay. We never store your card numbers, UPI IDs, or banking credentials. {% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans %} Access controls to limit internal access to personal data. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Note" %}: {% blocktrans trimmed %} Our database is hosted on Supabase in Mumbai, India. Supabase encrypts stored data at rest (storage-level encryption), and data is encrypted in transit over HTTPS. TrackMyRupee does not add a second layer of encryption inside the app, so the app itself, and authorised administrators of the database, can read your transaction data. Please avoid entering highly sensitive information, such as bank account or card numbers, in descriptions. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "6. Your Rights Under DPDP Act 2023" %}

{% blocktrans %} Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you have the right to: (1) Access your personal data. (2) Correct inaccurate data. (3) Request erasure of your data. (4) Withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account from Settings -> My Data -> Delete Account. Withdrawal of consent will result in account termination as the service cannot be provided without processing your data. (5) File a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India at dpboard.gov.in. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Note on Erasure Requests" %}: {% blocktrans %} Deleting your account removes your data from our live database. Some records are not removed: a minimal record of the deletion request, and data held by the service providers listed in section 4. Section 7 explains each of these. {% endblocktrans %}
