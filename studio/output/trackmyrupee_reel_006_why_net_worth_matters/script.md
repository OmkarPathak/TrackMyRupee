# Why seeing your net worth matters

## Strategy
- **Pillar:** B — Financial education (with a Pillar C product-proof close)
- **Audience:** Salaried professionals 22–35, SIP/mutual fund investors, people with money spread across several apps
- **Objective:** Teach one idea clearly (net worth = own − owe, and why it matters), then show the habit + one-place tracking that makes it effortless. Drive demo taps. Education first; the product appears in the last third.
- **Duration:** ≈48 seconds (8 scenes; windows follow the narration)
- **Format:** Calm-paper explainer, mascot narrating (Line mouth, audio-driven), real app captures (demo account), illustrative example labelled
- **Emotional Angle:** Curiosity ('am I really richer?') → clarity (one simple formula) → relief ('one place, tiny habit')
- **Viewer Reaction:** 'I've never added this up' → save it / send to a friend → try the demo
- **Feature:** Net worth (accounts list + net worth history, real), 34 Indian account types (real list), quick-add habit (real capture)
- **Conversion:** Free demo (link in bio → trackmyrupee.com/demo, no registration)
- **Why This Idea:** Net worth is the reframe that makes every other money habit matter. It is the clearest education→product bridge: the product's central number, explained before it is sold.

## Hooks
1. Same salary. Five years later, one friend is worth ten lakh more. Why?  ← selected
2. I'm a piggy bank, and I know exactly what's inside me: ₹5,000. Do you know what you're worth? (hook C)
3. Quick test. What's your net worth? You've got three seconds. (hook B)
4. Your salary went up. But are you actually richer? (v1 hook)
5. Earning more ≠ owning more. Here's the number that tells the truth.
6. You track your spending. Do you know what you're worth?
7. Your bank balance is not your net worth.
