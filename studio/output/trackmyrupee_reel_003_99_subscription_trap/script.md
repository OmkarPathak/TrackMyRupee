# The ₹99 subscription trap

## Strategy
- **Pillar:** A — Relatable money problems (with a Pillar C product close)
- **Audience:** Salaried professionals 22–35
- **Objective:** Shares/saves via a shocking-but-simple number; then product proof (recurring + reminders). Funnel: relatable → tip → free demo. Pairs with Friday's Hinglish carousel '10 expenses you might be forgetting'.
- **Duration:** ≈40 seconds (6 scenes; deliberately short)
- **Format:** Calm-paper explainer, mascot narrating (Line mouth, audio-driven), real app captures (demo account), illustrative maths labelled
- **Feature:** Subscriptions page (monthly/daily/yearly totals, Renewing Soon tab) · reminder three days before renewal — verified (real capture + FEATURES.md)
- **Conversion:** Free demo (link in bio, no registration)

## Hooks
1. Five plans. ₹99 each. So what's that a year?  ← selected
2. You pay ₹99 a month for something you forgot. Ever added it up?
3. ₹99 is nothing. ₹5,940 is something. Same money.
4. How many ₹99 plans are still billing you?
5. The ₹99 trap: it's never one plan.

_Number-led hook: the maths is the visual; payoff ('₹5,940 a year') lands inside 5 seconds._

## Illustrative maths (labelled on screen)
- 5 plans × ₹99 = ₹495 a month
- × 12 = **₹5,940 a year**

## Scene script

### Scene 1 · 0.0–4.8s · Hook (number-led)
- **On-screen:** ₹99 × 5 = ?
- **VO (EN):** Five plans. ₹99 each. So what's that a year?
- **SFX:** Ding, pop per symbol, thud on '?'
- **Transition:** Crisp fade-through

### Scene 2 · 4.8–12.1s · The reveal
- **On-screen:** ₹495 a month · ₹5,940 a year · 12 × ₹495
- **VO (EN):** ₹495 a month. ₹5,940 a year. All quietly renewing.
- **SFX:** Tick per tile, thud on the total
- **Transition:** Crisp fade-through

### Scene 3 · 12.1–16.9s · One you forgot
- **On-screen:** One of them you forgot. · Cloud storage ₹99 · still renewing
- **VO (EN):** And one of them? You've probably forgotten it's still running.
- **SFX:** Soft pops; low note on the highlight
- **Transition:** Crisp fade-through

### Scene 4 · 16.9–25.4s · Catch them in 3 steps
- **On-screen:** 1 Scan last month's statement · 2 List every recurring charge · 3 Note each renewal date
- **VO (EN):** Catch them in three steps. Scan last month's statement. List every recurring charge. Then note each renewal date.
- **SFX:** Tick per step
- **Transition:** Crisp fade-through

### Scene 5 · 25.4–34.4s · TrackMyRupee (real app)

- **SFX:** Whoosh, pops, chime
