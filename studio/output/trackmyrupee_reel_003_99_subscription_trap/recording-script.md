# Reel 3 — recording script (your voice)
Read each line as its own take (or one take with a 1.5 s pause between lines). Warm, a little wry, like telling a friend. Smile while you speak.

| # | Line | Delivery |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Five plans. Ninety-nine rupees each. So what's that a year?" | Punchy. Lift on "year?" and stop — leave a beat of silence after. |
| 2 | "Four hundred ninety-five rupees a month. Five thousand nine hundred forty rupees a year. All quietly renewing." | Calm. Pause before "Five thousand nine hundred forty", slow down on it, then a slightly dry "All quietly renewing." |
| 3 | "And one of them? You've probably forgotten it's still running." | Knowing, conversational. |
| 4 | "Catch them in three steps. Scan last month's statement. List every recurring charge. Then note each renewal date." | Steady, tick off each step with a small pause. |
| 5 | "In TrackMyRupee, add each one once. You see the monthly and yearly total, and get a reminder three days before it renews." | Friendly, clear. Slight emphasis on "once" and "three days before". |
| 6 | "No bank login. No S-M-S access. Try the free demo — link in bio." | Confident, light. Say S-M-S as letters. |

Tips: quiet room (soft furnishings help), phone voice memo ~15–20 cm from your mouth, no fan/AC, no music. Leave ~1 s of silence before and after each line. Two takes of each line is plenty. Any format (m4a / wav / mp3) is fine.
