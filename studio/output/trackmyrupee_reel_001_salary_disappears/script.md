# Where does your ₹50,000 salary disappear?

## Strategy
- **Pillar:** A — Relatable money problems
- **Audience:** Salaried professionals 22–35, IT professionals, young working professionals
- **Objective:** Shares and saves first (relatability), profile visits and demo taps second. Establish the TrackMyRupee look.
- **Duration:** 35 seconds (30–35s sweet spot; every beat earns its place)
- **Format:** Kinetic data motion-graphic: one salary drawn as 50 tiles (1 tile = ₹1,000) that get named group by group. Real app screenshot only in the solution beat.
- **Emotional Angle:** Recognition → mild shock ('₹8,000 with no name') → relief ('there is a simple fix')
- **Viewer Reaction:** 'This is literally me' → send to a friend / save for salary day
- **Feature:** Quick-add: type an expense like a message (documented example: “swiggy 320”) + the Top Categories dashboard (real screenshot)
- **Conversion:** Tap 'Free demo' link in bio → trackmyrupee.com/demo (no registration needed to explore)
- **Why First:** It is the broadest relatable hook in the library, needs no product explanation before delivering value, and the tile-grid device becomes a reusable brand visual for the Salary Diaries series.

## Hooks
1. ₹50,000 in. Gone by the 20th. Where?  ← selected
2. Your salary doesn't vanish. It leaks — ₹200 at a time.
3. Be honest: can you account for last month's ₹50,000?
4. Salary on the 1st. Panic on the 20th.
5. A ₹50,000 salary. ₹8,000 nobody can explain.
6. ₹50,000 came in. Can you name where it went?

_v2: the number is on screen from frame 0, drains to ₹0 in 0.9s, then two hard slams ('Gone by the 20th.' / 'Where?') with screen shake. Previous v1 hook (slow count-up) kept as option 6._

## Music
Minimal, warm, modern-Indian-urban lo-fi pulse, 96–104 BPM, no vocals. Soft tabla-style or muted-pluck texture is welcome but keep it understated. Low under the VO (-22 LUFS-ish bed), lift by ~3dB in the end card.

0–3s: just a pad and the notification ding. 3–17s: add a steady light pulse that follows the tile ticks. 15–17.6s: riser then drop out. 17.6–22s: near-silent (single low note). 22–31s: pulse returns, brighter. 31–35s: resolve on a major chord with the chime.
