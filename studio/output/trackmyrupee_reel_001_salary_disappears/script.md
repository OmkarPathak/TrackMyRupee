# Where does your ₹50,000 salary disappear?

## Strategy
- **Pillar:** A — Relatable money problems
- **Audience:** Salaried professionals 22–35, IT professionals, young working professionals
- **Objective:** Shares and saves first (relatability), then show why a tiny daily habit beats a monthly clean-up, then prove it with real app footage → demo taps.
- **Duration:** 47.5 seconds (hook 3.4s · salary story 19.7s · habit 7.2s · real app 13s · CTA 4.2s)
- **Format:** Calm paper-style motion graphic (modelled on the director's reference animation): warm off-white background, bold rounded type with coral accents, white cards with soft shadows, Rupee Minis mascot in the corner, dark subtitle pills, slow fade-through transitions, 60 fps. One salary drawn as 50 tiles (1 tile = ₹1,000) named group by group; a crop of the real app screenshot in the solution beat.
- **Emotional Angle:** Recognition → mild shock ('₹8,000 with no name') → relief ('there is a simple fix')
- **Viewer Reaction:** 'This is literally me' → send to a friend / save for salary day
- **Feature:** Quick-add (type a line, the form fills itself) · expense list with names/categories · optional 9 pm daily reminder — all from REAL app captures (demo data)
- **Conversion:** Tap 'Free demo' link in bio → trackmyrupee.com/demo (no registration needed to explore)
- **Why First:** It is the broadest relatable hook in the library, needs no product explanation before delivering value, and the tile-grid device becomes a reusable brand visual for the Salary Diaries series.

## Hooks
1. ₹50,000 in. Gone by the 20th. Where?  ← selected

## Music
Calm, minimal, warm — soft plucks/keys, very low in the mix (about −24 LUFS bed under the voice), no drops. The visual style is gentle, so keep the music gentle.

0–3s: just a pad and the notification ding. 3–17s: add a steady light pulse that follows the tile ticks. 15–17.6s: riser then drop out. 17.6–22s: near-silent (single low note). 22–31s: pulse returns, brighter. 31–35s: resolve on a major chord with the chime.
