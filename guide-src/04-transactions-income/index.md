# Adding Income

Log your inflows and categorize them to understand where your money is coming from.

1. **Opening the income form** — **Desktop:** **Add** (navbar) → **Add Income**. **Mobile:** **+** FAB → **Add Income**. Also accessible via **Sidebar → Income → + Add** (desktop).
2. **Choosing a Source Type** — the **Source Type** field has these exact options: **Salary**, **Freelance / Consulting**, **Business**, **Investment Returns**, **Rental Income**, **Cashback & Rewards**, **Refund / Reimbursement**, **Other**. Source type determines how the entry is grouped in analytics. Salary is your primary earned income; Investment Returns is passive; Refund / Reimbursement is a one-off recovery that shouldn't inflate your savings rate.
3. **Filling the form** — **Date**, **Account** (which account the money lands in), **Amount**, **Currency**, **Source Type**, **Description** (optional). If you want this to repeat, toggle **Mark as Recurring** and pick a **Frequency** — this creates a subscription that auto-posts the income going forward. Hit **Save**.
4. **Where it appears** — the entry appears in the **Income** list (`/income/list/`) grouped by source type, on the **Dashboard** Income tile, and in the **Analytics** charts.
5. **Editing or deleting** — find the entry in the Income list, click/tap it to open the edit form ('Edit Income'), make changes, and **Save**. To delete, use the delete action and confirm.

!!! example "Real-world use case"
    Vikram is a freelance UI designer. On the 1st he logs his ₹45,000 monthly retainer as **Source Type: Freelance / Consulting** — and toggles **Mark as Recurring** with **Monthly** frequency so it auto-posts every month. On the 20th he receives a one-off ₹10,000 project bonus and logs it manually as **Source Type: Freelance / Consulting** (without the recurring toggle). Keeping them both under the same source type but entered separately means his Income list clearly shows the regular retainer vs. the lumpy bonus — and the Analytics savings-rate chart doesn't treat the bonus month as 'normal'.
