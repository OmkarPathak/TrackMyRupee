# Emails from TrackMyRupee

Emails come from `no-reply@trackmyrupee.com`. This page lists each one, when it is sent, and how to stop the ones you can stop.

---

## Account emails

| Email | When | Notes |
|-------|------|-------|
| **Confirm your email address** | When you sign up, or ask for another from the verification banner | The link is single-use. You can ask for a new one once every 15 minutes, up to 3 a day. Other emails (such as the welcome email) do not count against that limit. |
| **Welcome** | Right after you sign up | A short note with a link to your dashboard. |
| **Password reset** | When you use *Forgot password* | The link is single-use. |
| **Account deleted** | After you delete your account | Confirms that your data is gone. |

If a confirmation email does not arrive, check spam, then use **Resend** on the banner. If delivery fails on our side, nothing is lost: ask for another.

---

## Getting-started tips (Free accounts)

Free accounts with a confirmed address get up to four short emails after sign-up:

| Day | Subject |
|-----|---------|
| 2 | 3 tips to master your finances |
| 5 | Recurring transactions |
| 14 | What Plus adds |
| 30 | Your first month in review (your own counts of expenses, income and categories) |

If our mail service was down for a few days, you get only the latest one that applies, not all of them at once. A failed send is retried on the next run. Paid accounts do not receive these.

---

## Alert digest (Plus and Pro)

One email a day that lists every new alert (bills due, budget alerts, goal milestones), or a single alert with its own title when there is only one. See [Notifications and Reminders](../16-notifications/index.md). Free accounts get no alert emails.

---

## Monthly report

Sent in the first days of each month to users with a confirmed address, covering the previous calendar month. It is skipped if you had no income or spending that month, and it is never sent twice for the same month.

How each figure is worked out:

| Figure | How |
|--------|-----|
| **Income** | Income in the month, excluding cashback and refunds |
| **Total Expenses (Outflow)** | Regular expenses + every loan repayment + capital events. This is a cash-out figure, so it is larger than what the savings rate counts. |
| **Savings** and **Savings rate** | The same definition as the dashboard: income − expenses − loan interest − capital events that count in averages, divided by income without cashback and refunds. Loan principal is repaying borrowed money, so it is not spending. |
| **Investments** | Balances of your investment accounts at the end of the month |
| **Credit Card Pending** | What you owe on your cards at the end of the month. A card in credit shows nothing. |
| **Net Worth Growth** | Your net worth at the end of the month compared with the day before the month began, both taken from your ledger |
| **Top Spending Categories** | Your four biggest categories of regular spending |
| **Smart Insight** | The share of your regular spending that went on your top category, and what 15% less on it would save |

## Controlling emails

- **Daily expense reminder** (push and in-app) can be turned off in **Settings → Profile**.
- You cannot yet switch off the monthly report, tips or announcements from settings. Write to support@trackmyrupee.com and we will turn them off for you.
